## Run and controls

Run `npm ci` (first checkout), then `npm run dev`; open http://127.0.0.1:5173/ and click the game to unlock audio.

- WASD: drive; mouse: requested aim. The actual barrel turns at its own bounded speed.
- Space: hold to charge, release to fire. Right mouse: cancel.
- Shift: slower movement. Stealth/sound suppression is a later milestone.
- T: toggle the marked incoming-fire test lane; R: reset the range.
- M: mute/unmute effects.

The stationary orange target near spawn is for tap/charge comparisons. The southern range has a patrolling target at y 600, x 600–900. Target HP resets three seconds after destruction. The training emitter at (420, 272) fires left every three seconds when enabled. Player death stops driving and cancels charge; R restores the range. This is a scripted fixture, not bot AI.

## What works

Worker-authoritative charge, reload, cancel/vent recoveries, charge-dependent mobility/damage/speed, HP/death/reset, and fixed shell range. Swept wall and moving-target collision chooses earliest impact, walls win ties, and damage accumulates before deaths so same-tick trades are valid. The muzzle firing segment is checked against cover. Fired shells survive death. Short button edges survive between ticks; a bounded inbox rejects duplicate/old sequence IDs and neutralizes overflow. Focus loss/stale movement input clears pending edges and cancels charge without firing.

Tank, turret, shells and moving targets interpolate for presentation while collision stays authoritative. Charge-scaled barrel recoil, muzzle flash and original synthesized shot effects distinguish tap/full shots. Confirmed impact/destruction rings appear at actual simulation locations; hit confirmation and player damage rings remain visible with audio muted. Vent has a sound/visual cue and an explicit HUD deadline. Transient events last one simulation tick, have monotonic IDs, and are consumed once; authority event output and client visual effects are capped at 64.
