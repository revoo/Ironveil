# M2 cannon laboratory — in progress

The first combat slice adds minimum-charge tap shots, 1.5-second charging, charge-scaled damage/speed and movement cost, cancellation, reload, and automatic venting. The Worker owns charge time, projectiles, target HP and target reset. Space holds/releases the cannon; right mouse cancels. Orange targets have health bars and reset three seconds after destruction. The ring and HUD show charging; the barrel determines shot direction. Focus loss and stale input cancel charging. Fast press/release edges are queued separately from movement.

Run `npm run dev` and open http://127.0.0.1:5173/. Aim at an orange target, tap or hold/release Space, or cancel with right mouse. Movement controls remain WASD and Shift.

Relative-motion sweeps catch targets that cross a shell path between ticks. Impact selection gives walls equal-time priority and resolves all same-tick impacts before applying damage, allowing trades and preventing shells from passing through an entity killed earlier in iteration order.
