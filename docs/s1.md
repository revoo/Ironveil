## What works

The default page starts a local Worker-hosted 2v2 Relay Control match: you and one allied bot against two enemy bots. All tanks use the same movement and cannon rules from M1/M2. Shots pass through allies; hull overlap is allowed. Damage accumulates before deaths, enabling trades. Fired shells survive the owner's death. Dead tanks respawn after five simulation seconds; two seconds of protection ends on firing or entering the relay, before damage resolution for that tick. Spawn selection considers actual enemy distance and wall sight lines.

One active 90 px relay awards one point per second to the only team present. Multiple teammates do not multiply scoring; contested or empty zones award none. Kills do not score. Three authored training-map sites rotate every 60 seconds, with the next site announced ten seconds ahead. First to 180 wins; otherwise the leader at seven minutes wins. A tie enters up to 60 seconds of sudden death, ending on the first uncontested scoring tick or a draw. Results freeze the match and offer Play again.

Pause/resume and restart buttons are available. Escape toggles pause. Losing document visibility auto-pauses; resume explicitly. Simulation ticks, AI, projectiles, score and respawn/protection timers freeze. Pending input is cleared; resuming a held fire key requires a fresh press. Restart restores the full match, actors and score. The old training range is available through its link, or `/?range=1`.

The owner-requested vector drift and faster direction-change experiment is recorded in [M1 tuning history](m1.md). It supersedes the original hull-alignment constraint for the current S1 playtest.

The HUD shows health, charge/recovery/vent timing, respawn countdown, objective distance, score and time. The minimap includes static cover, the active relay, teammates and authorized enemy sightings. Team markers include different shapes. Movement and shells remain interpolated for display. Impact audio/effects reuse the M2 presentation.

## Bots and information boundary

Bots decide at staggered 6 Hz with a roughly 0.33-second acquisition delay and seeded aim error. They navigate to the relay, acquire direct firing angles, choose short/long charges based on distance, release shots when aligned, cancel when sight is lost and replan when stuck. This is the first normal behavior baseline, not the complete final bot feature set.

Weighted four-neighbour A* uses terrain costs and inflated cover clearance; it cannot cut diagonal corners. Replanning is bounded by decision cadence. Tests and the map validator verify that every authored spawn can reach every relay site.

The decision function accepts an explicit team observation, its own bot memory, and static map data. It receives no world reference, enemy cannon objects or hidden transforms. For this milestone, vision is the union of living teammates' 440 px wall-unobstructed sight. Dead allies supply no sight. Enemy sprites, shells and events are filtered in the Worker before transmission. Hiding removes live targets and their interpolation history. Bots also require their own direct wall-clear firing line, so team knowledge does not make them fire through cover. Brush/smoke, sound evidence, stale sightings and fuller stealth behavior belong to S2.

## Run and controls

`npm ci`, then `npm run dev`; open http://127.0.0.1:5173/ and click the game for audio. Solo needs only Vite, not the Colyseus server. `npm run dev:all` starts the legacy development connection diagnostic as well.

- WASD drive; mouse aim; Space hold/release to charge/fire; right-click cancel.
- Shift slows movement; M mutes; Escape pauses.
- Restart match (or R) starts fresh; Play again appears at results.

For the playtest, complete a match and assess whether the bots create understandable, worthwhile encounters and whether the relay makes movement purposeful. Report boring waits, bots that appear unfair or inert, confusing deaths/score, navigation problems, and moments that made you want another encounter. These questions are for the complete milestone, not intermediate slice testing.

## Tests and diagnostics

Local checks: 51 unit tests, 13 browser acceptance tests, production build and map/route validation pass. Tests were written before the new match behavior, navigation, bot intent and solo controls; initial red checks covered missing match structure/scoring/vision/lifecycle, routing, navigation/fire intent and pause UI. Follow-up regression tests caught bots trying to attack through their own cover and protection lasting through a relay-entry impact. The fixes pass their tests. A stricter held-Space assertion also caught keyboard repeat recharging after resume: the earlier retrying assertion could mask it by waiting through vent recovery. The regression now checks immediately, and repeat keydowns are ignored.

Browser acceptance includes a real local match, pause/resume/restart, hidden-page pause, a full seeded browser Worker run and a separate controlled result-rendering fixture. The result fixture tests presentation only; it is not substituted for the real Worker simulation. Training-range regression checks remain intact. A visual check confirmed the rendered HUD, map, tank, minimap and relay.

`npm run diagnostics:solo -- 100` completed 100 seeds with an idle human: Red won all 100, 2,966 deaths occurred, at most five projectiles were active, and the longest measured bot stop away from the objective was 0.77 seconds. No nonfinite tank positions or unterminated matches were observed. This is a lifecycle/navigation smoke check, not proof of fairness, enjoyment or a desirable win rate. It does not prove recovery for every possible map or unreachable configuration.

Node and Chromium exhibit tiny transcendental rounding differences. A diagnostic whole-match exact-equality assertion failed because those differences eventually changed encounters. The final runtime check verifies the first 300 ticks within 1e-7, independently completed matches in both runtimes, and identical repeated browser-Worker replays. Full cross-runtime bit identity is not claimed or required; the Worker is the local match authority. Cross-runtime authoritative correction remains future online work.

No GitHub CI run was triggered; local verification was used pending owner playtest feedback. Existing bundle-size warning and dependency advisories remain documented limitations, not new performance/capacity claims.

## Decisions and limits

Normal behavior only; other difficulty presets, offline caching, tutorial/settings polish, production Relay Yard and stealth evidence are later milestones. The training layout is authored in shared content; Tiled import remains future map work. This is online-independent local play, not yet cached offline delivery. Basic sight filtering establishes bot fairness now; the full S2 stealth policy is not implied. Bots currently have simple objective/attack/recovery choices, without the later smoke/retreat/flank sophistication. No multiplayer work was added. Protocol/content versions are 2 for the new observation and solo layout.
